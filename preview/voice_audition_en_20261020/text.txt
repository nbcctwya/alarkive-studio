I used to treat the world like a tower I had to climb. Every book had to make me smarter. Every hobby had to turn into something useful. Even rest felt like preparation for more work.

Lately, I've been trying a different question: what if the world could be my playground? I could make something just because I'm curious. Be a beginner. Try, change my mind, and try again. I still want to do things well. I just want to enjoy being here, too.
